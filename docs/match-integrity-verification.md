## Implementation and operational limits

- `ProtectedMatch` samples each effective PvP second at most once per team pair, matches reciprocal activity against the same independent side within five seconds, and excludes allies. `KothListeners` observes MONITOR damage without changing MaceGuard rules. Control evidence requires both controllers to have scored. Combat samples are bounded to 3600 per pair; pattern scans cover at most the newest 500 public match decisions in the configured window. Flags are investigation prompts, not proof of shared humans.
- Holds commit frozen qualification, progression configuration, protection-policy audit and evidence before awards. `/ekoth holds`, `/ekoth reports <match UUID>` and `/ekoth reviewmatch <UUID> approve|reject <evidence>` require `enthusiakoth.admin`. Held matches get no challenge, pool, relic, tag or guild XP claims. Approval uses original qualifications/policy, checks daily reservations atomically and is once-only; rejection cannot later approve. Currency/XP uncertain-payout reconciliation remains separate.
- UTC ceilings reserve event currency and maximum item-choice shares on the match completion date before redemption. Held events reserve only on approval; rejected events consume none. Reservations never reset on reconnect/choice/restart and never exceed the fixed event pool. Unused clipped shares are not redistributed. Known older paid/pending budgets are backfilled conservatively. Tags, lifetime relics and separately configured guild XP are outside these event ceilings.
- Arena names and world/objective-center geometry (milliblock precision) are persisted as aliases. Equal centers share an identity even across different modes/radii or a shared warzone region. Name/geometry collisions merge conservatively and historical totals resolve aliases. Enabled loaded arenas are registered at startup/reload before edits; disabled setup drafts are not registered. Previously deleted unknown geometries cannot be reconstructed. Existing earned rewards are not revoked by later identity merges. Staff must accept real arena diversity; nearby displaced copies cannot be identified as the same physical hill automatically.
- Readiness is opt-in and checks protection, alliance roster, loaded world/WorldGuard provider, economic provider when needed, accepted arena IDs and configured reward definitions before payment/flare consumption, scheduled starts and delayed activation. Current Tags `getRegistry().get(id)` is verified against companion source and read via a fail-closed optional lookup. LoreItems V1 has only `queueDelivery`, no definition query: ANY configured LoreItems reward definition blocks enforced readiness until a read-only provider query becomes available. No speculative delivery is used to probe a template. Signature authenticity, textures and MaceGuard rotation coverage still require manual TEST acceptance; `accepted-arenas` records that acceptance, it does not prove coverage automatically.
- `/ekoth results` records each observed hill participant, including zero-scoring participants, and shows their scoring, qualification decision, frozen floors and links to their claims/current challenge progress. It exposes no other players' audit/exclusion details. Staff report and uncertain claim permissions remain separately checked on every command/menu click.
